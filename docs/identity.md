# Product identity

Mechanism signature: a frozen software-release baseline, public advisory scan, timed quarantine, evidence-based patch review, and independent human clearance.

Product metaphor: a railway signal cabin. The release moves along one track; quarantine drops a physical red semaphore across it.

Voice: terse operational dispatches. Sentences behave like control-room instructions, not case-law prose.

Interface grammar: one horizontal rail, a movable signal arm, an inspection drawer, and a compact telemetry strip. No card grid and no repeated URL columns.

Evidence story: baseline documents are pinned before the train moves. Live advisories can stop it. Patch proof can prepare clearance, but the designated reviewer owns the final green signal.
